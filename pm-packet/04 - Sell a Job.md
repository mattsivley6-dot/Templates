# Sell a Job

## 1. The call comes in
- Get the address, the contact, the problem, and when they need it.
- Make a pre-booking folder in your job folder root: `<CUSTOMER>-<Scope>`.

## 2. Walk the site
Bring: phone, tape, flashlight, ladder access, voice recorder.
- Video the whole walk. Talk while you film: what you see, what you think it needs.
- Photograph every data plate. Model, serial, voltage, tonnage.
- Photograph the roof access, the crane path, the electrical panel, the condensate path, the thermostat.
- Measure the curb, the duct connections, the line set run.
- Ask: who approves, who pays, when do they need it, any after-hours rules.

## 3. Scope it
- Write the scope in plain words. What you replace, what you reuse, what you exclude.
- Exclusions always: electrical unless priced, roofing, structural, permits unless priced, controls unless priced.
- Send the scope to the customer before you price. "Is this what you want?"

## 4. Price it
- Equipment quote from the vendor, in writing.
- Sub quotes in writing: electrical, crane, roofing.
- Labor: hours x your burden rate.
- Add PM hours. Public work needs more.
- Apply your company's markup and check margin against your company floor. Ask your manager if you do not know them.

## 5. Send the proposal
- Build the quote in BuildOps. Quote number goes on everything.
- Attach the scope. State what is excluded. State lead times.
- Follow up in 3 business days. Then weekly.

## 6. When they say yes
- Get it signed, or get their PO.
- Go to 05 - Run a Job.

## Public bids (ISD, county, federal)
- Use `../checklists/TX Public-Entity Bid - DQ Prevention Checklist.md`.
- Every form, every signature, every addendum acknowledged.
- Bid bond if required. Submit 2 hours early.
