# Close a Job

Closeout is 3 lists. Start them the day the equipment ships, not the day the job ends.

## List 1: What the customer gets
- [ ] As-built or record drawings (marked up, then clean).
- [ ] O&M manuals for every unit.
- [ ] Warranty letters: manufacturer and ours. Start dates in writing.
- [ ] Startup reports.
- [ ] Test and balance report, if in scope.
- [ ] Controls documentation, if in scope.
- [ ] Training sign-in sheet.
- [ ] Spare parts and filters delivered, with a signed receipt.
- [ ] Permits closed. Final inspection passed.
- [ ] Closeout letter.

Owner's projects (ISD, public) have their own list. Get it in writing before you start. Use their forms.

## List 2: What we need from subs
- [ ] Final invoice.
- [ ] Final lien waiver.
- [ ] Their warranty letter.
- [ ] Their as-builts or record drawings.
- [ ] Their O&Ms.

Chase weekly. Hold their final payment until the list is done.

## List 3: What we do inside
- [ ] Final change order signed.
- [ ] Final pay app with retainage sent.
- [ ] All POs received and billed. None in Draft.
- [ ] Warranty dates in BuildOps.
- [ ] Job folder complete. Freeze it.
- [ ] Cost vs bid reviewed with your manager.
- [ ] Lessons written (3 lines minimum) in `forms/Lessons Register.xlsx`.
- [ ] BuildOps status set to Work Complete. Tell [Billing/AP] the final pay app is ready to invoice.

## Keep one status file
`<job folder>\_CLOSEOUT STATUS.md`. One table: item, status, proof, owner. Update it in place. Never start over.
