# PM Packet

A short field guide for an HVAC project manager: sell, run, close and get paid on mechanical jobs.

## Who you are
You are a Project Manager for an HVAC contractor ([Company DBA]). You sell, run and close HVAC jobs.

This packet is written for a shop that runs jobs in BuildOps. If you use another job platform, read "BuildOps" as "your job system."

## The packet
| File | What it is for |
|---|---|
| 01 - Rules | The 7 rules. Read first. |
| 02 - Job Lifecycle | The 10 phases and the checklist for each. |
| 03 - BuildOps How To | Habits that keep your job system honest. |
| 04 - Sell a Job | Site walk to signed proposal. |
| 05 - Run a Job | Setup, ordering, crew, field problems. |
| 06 - Get Paid | POs, pay apps, invoices, change orders. |
| 07 - Close a Job | Closeout in 3 lists. |
| 09 - Daily and Weekly Routine | Your day, your week. |
| 10 - Templates | The job folder scheme and where the forms are. |
| 11 - Job State Card | The one-page card that goes in every job folder. |

To map roles at your company, fill in `forms/RACI.xlsx`.

## Where things are
- Forms: `forms/` in this folder (prefunctional, startup, training, punch list, acceptance, RACI, communications plan, lessons register).
- Checklists: `../checklists/` in this repo.
- Document generators: `../template-library/templates/` in this repo.
- Job folders: `<job folder root>` on your company drive. Scheme in 10 - Templates.
- Tracker: your own job tracker. One row per job.

## Your first day
1. Read 01 and 02.
2. Get a BuildOps login. Ask [Project Admin].
3. Open the tracker. Every job you own has a row.
4. Read the Job State Card in each job folder you own.
5. Ask your manager what is due this week.
