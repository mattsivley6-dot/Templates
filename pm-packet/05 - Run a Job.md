# Run a Job

## Day 1 after the win
1. Create the job in BuildOps. Write the number down.
2. Rename the folder: `<NUMBER>-<CUSTOMER>-<Scope>`.
3. Make the numbered subfolders (see 10 - Templates).
4. Put the Job State Card in `_RUNNING NOTES.txt` (see 11).
5. Add the tracker row. Set a target date.
6. Fill your company's handoff form (transition package). Hold the handoff meeting.
7. Build the Booking Package: `20-Submission Packets\<NUMBER>_<Short>_Booking Package` with 01-Equipment, 02-Proposal, 03-Submittals, 04-Estimate, 05-Transition. Copies of primary-source docs only (vendor quotes and O&Ms, our quote and the customer's acceptance, submittals, the estimate, the transition package). Nothing gets written for it.

## Order it
1. Confirm the equipment matches what was sold. Model, voltage, handedness, curb.
2. Submittal to the customer if they require one. Wait for approval.
3. PO in BuildOps, issued, not Draft. Job name and number on the PO.
4. Email the vendor: PO number, job name, ship-to, need-by date.
5. Ship date in the tracker. Chase it weekly.
6. When it lands: photograph it, check it against the PO, receive it in BuildOps as Equipment.

## Schedule it
1. Crew: ask [Scheduler] for dates. Confirm in writing.
2. Crane and lift: book 2 weeks out. COI to the customer.
3. Customer: confirm the date, the hours, the access, the shutdown.
4. Electrician and other subs: date confirmed in writing.
5. Text the crew the night before: address, start time, what to bring.

## While they work
- Be there at start. Walk the plan with the lead.
- Daily: photos to `30-Photos and Videos`, hours and problems in the running notes.
- Any change: stop, price it, get it approved in writing, then do it.
- Any hidden condition: photo, written notice to the customer the same day.

## Field problems
| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| Wrong voltage or wrong unit | Stop. Photo the nameplate. Call the vendor for an exchange before you buy anything. |
| Condensate has no path | Price a pump or a re-route as a change order. Do not improvise. |
| Customer adds work | "Happy to. I will send a price." Nothing starts without a signed price. |
| Sub does not show | Call, then email. Log it. Have a backup sub name ready. |
| Crew ran over hours | Find out why the same day. Note it in the running notes. |

## Every Friday
- Update the tracker row.
- Update the Job State Card.
- Tell the customer where things stand, in 3 lines.
