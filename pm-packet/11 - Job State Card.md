# Job State Card

Goes at the top of `_RUNNING NOTES.txt` in every job folder. Update it every Friday and whenever something changes. Anyone can pick up the job from this card.

```
==== JOB STATE: <Job name> ====
Job #: <number> | Customer: <name, address>
Status: <Quoted / Approved / Ordered / In Progress / Complete / Closeout> | Phase: <1-10>
Next action: <one sentence>
Ball in court: <name>
Target date: <date> | Tracker last updated: <date>
Contract: $<amount> | Billed: $<amount> | Unbilled: $<amount>
Open tasks:
- <task> | waiting on: <name> | due: <date>
==== END JOB STATE ====
```

Below the card: running notes, newest on top. Example:

```
2026-01-15
- RTU-1 set. Rusted duct at the curb, photo in 30-Photos and Videos. Priced fix sent to Sam Roe (owner rep).
- [Billing/AP] told: ready to invoice $10,000.00.
```

## Rules
- Never delete old notes.
- Quotes from calls get the time: "Sam Roe 10:40: approved the fix."
- If the card and the tracker disagree, fix both today.
