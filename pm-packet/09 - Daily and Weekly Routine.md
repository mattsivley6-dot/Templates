# Daily and Weekly Routine

## Every morning (15 minutes)
1. Read your morning briefing or the tracker.
2. What is due today and tomorrow? Do those first.
3. Anyone waiting on you? Answer before 9.
4. Text the crew if they start somewhere new.

## Every day
- Answer customer and sub email the same day.
- Photos and notes from the field go in the job folder the same day.
- New promise to anyone: put it in the tracker with a date.

## Every Friday (30 minutes)
1. Update every tracker row you own. Target date, next action, ball in court.
2. Update every Job State Card.
3. 3-line status to each active customer.
4. Move finished tasks to Archive.

## Every Monday (30 minutes)
1. Billing email to [Billing/AP]: finished work not invoiced, sub invoices released, POs in Draft.
2. Chase every open ship date.
3. Chase every open sub quote.
4. Check BuildOps for Draft POs.

## Every month
1. Pay app on every project.
2. Cost vs budget on every project.
3. Closeout lists moved forward on every job past Phase 7.

## End of every job
- Cost vs bid with your manager.
- 3 lessons in the register.
