# The 7 Rules

1. **Get it in writing.** Every scope, price, date and approval. A phone call is not a record. Follow it with an email.

2. **The tracker is the truth.** Update your row the day something changes. Every job gets a target date.

3. **Every job has a number.** No job number, no work. Get the number before you order or schedule.

4. **Every document traces to a source.** Quantity, price, date, model. If you cannot point to where it came from, do not send it.

5. **Never invent a form.** Use the owner's form. If you do not have it, ask for it.

6. **Money follows paper.** No PO, no order. No signed change order, no extra work. No pay app, no payment.

7. **Bill the week the work finishes.** Tell [Billing/AP] the same day. Waiting costs the company money.

## How to write
- Short. Plain words. Like an attorney talking to a 12 year old.
- Lead with the ask.
- One email, one purpose.
- Sign with your own name and phone number.
