# Get Paid

## Small jobs (quoted)
1. Work finishes.
2. Same day: email [Billing/AP]. Job number, amount, "ready to invoice."
3. [Billing/AP] invoices. Ask for the invoice number.
4. Follow up on payment at 30 days.

## Projects (SOV and pay apps)
1. SOV approved before the first pay app.
2. Monthly: build the pay app in BuildOps.
3. Export the pay application and its continuation sheet. Sign. Notarize if required.
4. Attach lien waivers from every sub paid last month.
5. Send to the owner's rep. Tell [Billing/AP] it went out.
6. Retainage is billed on the final pay app with the closeout package.

## Change orders
1. Something changes. Stop.
2. Price it: labor, material, sub, markup. Attach the sub's quote.
3. Send the PCO. Get it signed.
4. Enter it in BuildOps with margin, not at cost.
5. Only then do the work.
6. Add it to the next pay app.

## Sub invoices
1. Sub sends an invoice. Check it against the PO and the work done.
2. If it is right, tell [Billing/AP] "approved to pay" with the PO number.
3. If it is wrong, tell the sub what to fix.
4. [Billing/AP] does not pay until you release it.
5. Vendors bill the company AP mailbox, never you. The office enters the invoice, [Billing/AP] pays.

## Card charges
- If you put a job cost on a card, send [Billing/AP] the receipt that day, with the job number.

## POs
- Every Monday: open BuildOps, filter POs to Draft. Issue or delete them. Draft POs hide real cost.

## The rule
Bill the week the work finishes. A job that is done and not invoiced is a loan to the customer.
