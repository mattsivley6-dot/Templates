# The 10 Phases

Every job runs through these in order. Check the box before you move on. The full list is `../checklists/Project Checklist - Start to Finish.docx`.

## Phase 1: Before you bid
- [ ] Read the whole solicitation. Log the due date, walk date and question deadline.
- [ ] Walk the site. Video everything. Photograph every data plate.
- [ ] Get quotes from vendors and subs in writing.
- [ ] Check addenda before you price.
- [ ] Submit 2 hours early.

## Phase 2: You won it
- [ ] Get the signed contract or PO.
- [ ] Create the job in BuildOps. Get the number.
- [ ] Make the job folder: `<NUMBER>-<CUSTOMER>-<Scope>`.
- [ ] Add the row to the tracker with a target date.
- [ ] Fill your company's handoff form (transition package).
- [ ] Hold the handoff meeting with sales ([Estimator]), [Ops Manager] and [Scheduler].
- [ ] Check the submittal equipment against what was sold.

## Phase 3: Before work starts
- [ ] Pre-construction meeting with the customer. Get their site rules.
- [ ] Baseline schedule sent to the customer.
- [ ] Schedule of values (SOV) approved if it is a project.
- [ ] Sub scopes in writing. Sub COI on file.
- [ ] POs issued for equipment and subs. Not in Draft.
- [ ] Kickoff with the crew.

## Phase 4: Submittals and ordering
- [ ] Submittal log started.
- [ ] Submittals sent and approved before you order.
- [ ] Long lead items ordered first. Ship dates in the tracker.
- [ ] Chase every ship date weekly.

## Phase 5: Permits and mobilizing
- [ ] Permits pulled. Inspections scheduled.
- [ ] Existing conditions photographed before you touch anything.
- [ ] Model and serial of every unit going out, photographed.
- [ ] Crane and lift booked. Certificate of insurance to the customer.

## Phase 6: While the work is going
- [ ] Daily report every day. Photos every day.
- [ ] RFI for every question. Written notice for every concealed condition.
- [ ] Priced change order before the extra work starts.
- [ ] Pay app every month.
- [ ] Walk the sub's work before you sign off.
- [ ] Check cost against budget once a month.

## Phase 7: Making it work
- [ ] Prefunctional Checklist filled before startup (`forms/Prefunctional Checklist.docx`).
- [ ] Startup by the manufacturer. Startup Checklist with readings in the folder (`forms/Startup Checklist.docx`).
- [ ] Test and balance. Controls checked.
- [ ] Filters changed. Owner trained. Sign-in sheet (`forms/Owner Training Record.docx`).

## Phase 8: Substantial completion
- [ ] Punch list walk with the customer (`forms/Punch List.docx`).
- [ ] Punch list done. Site clean.
- [ ] Acceptance Form signed (`forms/Acceptance Form.docx`), or the owner's own form.

## Phase 9: Closeout
- [ ] See 07 - Close a Job.

## Phase 10: After the job
- [ ] Retainage billed and collected.
- [ ] Warranty dates in BuildOps.
- [ ] Cost vs bid reviewed. Lessons written.
- [ ] Job set to Work Complete in BuildOps.
