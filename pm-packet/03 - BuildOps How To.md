# BuildOps How To

Habits for a PM working in BuildOps or any field-service and project platform. Your admin sets your permissions; learn what your role can and cannot change before you need it.

## Do it right
1. **Issue the PO before you order.** Draft POs do not count. Check for Draft POs every Monday.
2. **Receive equipment as Equipment,** not Material. Job cost depends on it.
3. **Put a labor budget on every project.** Without it the system cannot warn you of an overrun.
4. **Price change orders with margin.** Do not leave them at cost.
5. **Log RFIs and submittals in the system,** not just in PDFs.
6. **Write daily reports in the system** on projects.

## Accounting sync
If your platform syncs to your accounting software, ask [Billing/AP] before you change the value of a synced PO or change order.
